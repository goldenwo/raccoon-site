Raccoon Heist

A raccoon crew pulls heists while you are away and stacks the loot in their den. You come back, crack the crates open, gear up, and send the crew after a bigger score.

  • Idle: the den fills up while the game is closed.
  • Crates of five tiers, gear of six rarities, a boss every ten stages.
  • For adults (18+). No purchases in this version.

Here's the math: crates contain random items, and the chance of every rarity in every crate is published, the same for every player. See the odds.
