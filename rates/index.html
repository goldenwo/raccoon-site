Odds

Every crate in Raccoon Heist contains random gear. These are the chances, per open, for every crate. They are the same for every player, and they are generated from the same tables the game server draws from, so this page cannot drift from the game. The game shows the same numbers under MORE > DROP RATES.

Items drop at your stage's level.

Item stats

Item stats (gear_stats v2, in effect from 2026-10-19 UTC): every item gives attack, its item power, plus one stat.

Below account level 8, every item's stat is bonus attack. From level 8, each item's stat is drawn from its slot's table, independent of the item's rarity and of every other roll, and a crit item's step is drawn from its rarity's band for that stat, independent of its slot and item level.

The level-8 unlock is the same for every player at that level.

Hat (from level 8): CRIT DMG 50.0000%, BONUS ATK 25.0000%, CRIT CHANCE 25.0000%.

Mask (from level 8): CRIT CHANCE 50.0000%, BONUS ATK 25.0000%, CRIT DMG 25.0000%.

Gloves (from level 8): CRIT CHANCE 50.0000%, BONUS ATK 25.0000%, CRIT DMG 25.0000%.

Boots (from level 8): CRIT DMG 50.0000%, BONUS ATK 25.0000%, CRIT CHANCE 25.0000%.

Tool (from level 8): BONUS ATK 50.0000%, CRIT CHANCE 25.0000%, CRIT DMG 25.0000%.

Attack on every item: its item power, Common 10, Uncommon 16, Rare 26, Epic 45, Legendary 80, Mythic 150 at item level 1, × 1.016 per item level.

BONUS ATK: 0.5 × item power, Common +5, Uncommon +8, Rare +13, Epic +22.5, Legendary +40, Mythic +75 at item level 1; it grows with the item's level, and a held item's value always follows the current factor.

A crit value grows with neither the item's level nor its slot's level; a held item's value always follows the current band at its step.

No item stores its value: a change to a factor or to a band's values changes the value of every held item it covers, with a new gear_stats version and a changelog entry saying so.

Crit damage stops at ×2.24 in total; crit chance stops at 100%.

Attack scale k = 1.3: in-game ATK = item attack × slot bonus × k (the bonus-attack factor applies to the unscaled item power; crit values are not scaled).
